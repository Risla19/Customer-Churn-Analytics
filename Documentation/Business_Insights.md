# Customer Churn – Business Insights

## 1. Overall Churn
- Total customers: 7,043
- Churned customers: 1,869
- Overall churn rate: 26.54%

## 2. Key Findings

### Contract Type
Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71%.

### Internet Service
Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of 41.89%.

### Payment Method
Electronic check users have the highest churn rate at 45.29%.

### Customer Tenure
Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn. The 0–12 month group has a churn rate of 47.44%.

### Technical Support
Customers without technical support have a higher churn rate (41.64%) than customers with technical support (15.17%).

## 3. Business Recommendations
- Encourage month-to-month customers to consider longer-term contracts.
- Investigate customer concerns related to fiber optic services.
- Review the payment experience for electronic check users.
- Improve onboarding and engagement for new customers.
- Promote technical support services to customers who do not currently use them.
